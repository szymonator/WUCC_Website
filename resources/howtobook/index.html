Booking Club Hours

Once you've joined and completed your induction, follow this step-by-step guide to book onto our free club sessions. You can see the current club hours slots here.

1. Search for Session

Head over to Warwick Sport's booking portal, and login.

Note that your login credentials may be different to the ones used for ITS.

Search for "Climbing - Club Session" in the search box, and click "Search".

2. Choose a date and time

Scroll down to see the list of possible sessions and pick whichever you like.

-- placeholder image --

3. Confirm Booking

Click 'Book now' to confirm your booking! You should get a confirmation email for your session.
