North Wales - 2026

October 2026 | Szymon Galutowski

Dates: October 17th - 18th, 2026

Location: Dinorwig, North Wales

The advanced lead trip to North Wales was a huge success! We had a lovely evening and somewhat grim briefing on Friday, and then on Saturday most of us were up bright and early to hit the crags. Most of us went to Never Never Land, with a few of us even doing some trad and multipitch! Saturday evening was spent enjoying a very tasty pasta, and the rest of the evening and the morning was a big rush to get everything cleaned up in time. On Sunday moring, we all went to Australia, once again having a go at some cool climbs. Wonderful weather, beautiful views, 10/10 would go again!
