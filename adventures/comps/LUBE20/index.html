LUBE 2019/20

November 3, 2019 | Cait Clement

The London University Bouldering Event (LUBE) is a yearly competition consisting of four rounds across terms 1 and 2. In week 4, we took a fantastic group of 15 WUCC climbers to Croywall to test their skills against the other 19 competing universities in round one! It was a lovely day, with a great atmosphere and lots of smiles from the Warwick team.

Round one saw two Warwick climbers in the Finals!! As well as Warwick coming second so far in the overall university rankings! Congratulations to everyone who competed – you were all amazing!

Bring on Round Two!
