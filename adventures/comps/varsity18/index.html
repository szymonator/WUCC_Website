Varsity 2018

February 3, 2018 | WUCC

2018 saw the first ever (unofficial) Warwick Uni vs Coventry Uni Varsity Bouldering Competition at The Ballroom, Coventry. Climbers had from 12 until 6 to attempt 25 problems ranging from very easy to really quite hard. The top two male scores and the top female scores were combined to give the totals for each university.

Team Warwick (733)
James Mabon - 274
Augustin Moinat - 266
Niamh Gibson - 193

Team Coventry (644)
Tatsuki Sato - 270
James Mead - 214
Bex Smith - 160
