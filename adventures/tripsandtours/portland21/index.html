Portland Tour - 2021

October 2, 2021 | James Wilson

Dates: 5th - 14th September, 2021

Location: Isle of Portland, Dorset, UK

Emerging from the COVID-19 pandemic, we were finally able to get out and about for some climbing together after last year's tour couldn't go ahead.

Portland is an amazing place that has many great climbing locations with climbs of varying difficulties - there is something for everyone! We stayed near the south of the Isle, within walking distance of many crags and the weather held up almost the whole time. We visited Blacknor (North, Central, Beach, South), Neddyfields, the Cuttings, Beeston, Battleship, Coastguards, Fallen Slab as well as a few pubs!

Thanks to Alban and Imi for organising it, it was so good to get back outside, see you on the next trip!
