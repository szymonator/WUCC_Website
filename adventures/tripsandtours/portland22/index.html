Portland Tour - 2022

August 23, 2022 | Samuel Bass

Dates: 8th - 10th August, 2022

Location: Isle of Portland, Dorset, UK

In the 3rd installment of our old-new exec handover trip, we travelled to the now familiar Portland bunkhouse to spend a few days bonding, climbing and teaching our new socials how to run a circle. Although short, we found the time to red point, swim and have a barbecue on our second night.

A big thanks to all those involved in the organisation, and good luck to all the exec leaving us this year and those joining the team for 2022/23
