Siurana, Spain - 2023

June 17th, 2023 | Samuel Bass

Dates: 20th March - 29th March, 2023

Location: Siurana, Tarragona, Spain

This year's took us to Siurana; one of Spain's, and even Europe's, most beautiful climbing hotspots. It is an incredible mountain spot with hundreds of incredible routes all found on either side of a picturesque valley

We were lucky to have some lovely weather during the day and all get our dose of Spanish sunlight, and at night our tour secretarie Rob Sleet and Pavel Scerbakovs kept us entertained (and quiet) with a barbecue and cocktail night

And let's not forget about the climbing! We all (except our injured few) had our fill of spanish climbing in the 10 days we there and experience some breathtaking fews of Siurana valley at the top of some of the most iconics climbs in Spain.

Apart from the climbing, we spent our rest days relaxing at the campsite and enjoying the wonderful food (fuet in particluar) or going on a short hike to the river in the valley and going for a refreshing swim.

Overall, 2023's tour was a huge success and we all had an incredible time forming memories which will surely last us a lifetime.
