We participate in bouldering and lead competitions both internally and externally all year round, representing the university climbing team as well as individual competitions

Sport
Bouldering
Speed

The climbing wall is located on campus in the new Sports and Wellness Hub and features a 15 metre comp wall with overhangs of up to 6 metres, 3 auto belays, roughly 100 boulder problems and an Olympic standard speed wall.

Travel
Discover
Explore

We run regular bouldering, trad and sport trips to all parts of the UK including the Peak District, Wales and the South West. There is also an annual Tour every Easter to a warmer climate, with past destinations including Sicily, Turkey, Sardinia and Spain.

Welcome to Warwick University Climbing Club

Welcome to the Warwick University Climbing Club website. We welcome everyone from complete novices to extreme pros. Watch our San Vito Lo Capo 2022 Tour video and others on our YouTube channel to get a taste of what we get up to on campus and outdoors, then have a browse to find out what else the club has to offer!
