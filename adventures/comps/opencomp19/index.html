Open Comp 2019

March 6, 2019 | WUCC

The annual WUCC open climbing competition consists of two rounds - a bouldering round and a lead climbing round. The bouldering round consists of 20 problems of varying difficulty and style with a maximum of three attempts. The lead round consists of 3 routes for male and female competitors with only one attempt to get as far as possible without resting or falling.

Results:
Male 1st: Sylvan Chouhan & Callum Holmes
Male 2nd: Ed Brown
Male 3rd: Will Douglas


Female 1st: Niamh Gibson
