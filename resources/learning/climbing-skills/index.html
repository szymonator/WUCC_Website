Climbing Skills

August 3, 2026 | Sidney Sherborne

Safety First: Skills should always be practiced thoroughly with somebody who knows what they’re doing before trying it on your own. Make sure you are signed off by the club before attempting to lead climb or belay without supervision.

Before you begin, check out the UK Climbing Glossary of Climbing Terminology to familiarize yourself with the vocabulary.
