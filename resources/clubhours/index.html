What are Club Hours?

Club hours are the sixteen hours a week during which a member of the Climbing Club can climb for free at the Sports Hub. At all club hours sessions (unless marked otherwise), members can hire gear (shoes, harness, belay device, etc.) from the club for free.

Unlike most sport clubs, club hours don't have a formal program, we just turn up and climb. The entire club is very friendly and if you look lost enough someone will most likely come over and say hi. Of course you can always come and say hi first!

The most comonly visited club hours are Wednesday afternoon and Friday afternoon, so book early if you want to climb with us!

To climb during club hours, book on the Warwick Sport website, there are instructions for this on our how to book page.
