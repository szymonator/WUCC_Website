About The Club

Welcome everyone to the largest sports club at Warwick!

We promote a friendly and inclusive atmosphere that welcomes students of all abilities; whether you are trying climbing for the first time, want to compete at a national level or simply want to enjoy our socials, we have something for you! Our aim is to ensure climbing remains as accessible as possible, with our membership providing the cheapest way to make use of the exceptional climbing facilities at Warwick Sport.

We regularly organise outdoor trips across the UK, visiting areas such as the Peak District and Snowdonia. Catering for all different types and levels of climbing, we ensure plan for these trips to occur frequently to maximise opportunities for our members to experience the joy of climbing on real rock. In addition, every year after the end of term 2, we take over 50 students to a world class European destination (previously Sicily, Siurana and El Chorro) for 10 days of outdoor lead climbing. These trips allow our members the freedom to explore many exceptional crags and are a fantastic way to meet like-minded people.

For those newer to the sport, we hold tasters during both welcome week and the first 2 weeks of term. These are free of charge and provide the perfect opportunity to try different types of climbing together with others new to the club. Since this is under the supervision of our exec it is also a great way to get to know us, and speak to more experienced climbers at the wall. Furthermore, for those unfamiliar with roped climbing we hold top rope and lead lessons during terms 1 and 2, ensuring all our members can safely master these skills and enjoy all our wonderful trips regardless of previous climbing experience.

If the idea of competing against other universities sounds appealing, this is something we do in abundance. Following trials at the start of the year, the team take part in a range of competitions across varying levels of intensity including BUCS, the Midlands University Climbing Competition, and the London University Climbing Competition. Throughout the year, we also hold our own intra-club competitions where everyone can take part. WUCC also organises and hosts the National Universities Lead and Speed Climbing Championships! You will have the opportunity to compete, volunteer in organisation and judging, or spectate.

Finally, we hold weekly drinking and sober socials that cater to everyone. Whether you want to socialise on bar crawls or circle for POP! (the SU club), these events are a great way to meet others whether you drink or not. As far as sober socials these include watching films and hosting board game nights, providing a great opportunity to relax from your studies and meet fellow climbers. If any of this sounds exciting, you can learn more on our Instagram (@w_u_c_c) or Whatsapp, where you can find further information about the things we get up to. We look forward to meeting everyone throughout the year and hope we can spread our passion for the sport which we love so much!

Lots of presidential love, Albane and Dan
