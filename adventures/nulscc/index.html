What is NULSCC?

Organised annually by Warwick University Climbing Club, the National University Lead and Speed Climbing Championship brings together university teams, solo competitors, and elite student athletes from across the UK for a high-energy showcase of roped climbing.

NULSCC Facts

  • The UK's largest student-led lead & speed competition
  • Non-profit, raising money for Climbers Against Cancer
  • One day event, with the best UK student climbers competing
  • Team and individual rankings across Open & Female categories
  • Previous years have seen GB team athletes and Olympians

2 disciplines

LEAD

One route. A time limit. The climber who reaches highest wins.

SPEED

Two climbers. One wall. Fastest to the top wins.
