Why join?

Joining our club is a great opportunity for all those who are passionate about climbing and for those who are interested in trying a new and unique sport. We're really looking forward to meeting all the new members whether you are a seasoned climber or if you've never stepped foot in a climbing wall.

We run regular events, such as:

Socials

We run a variety of drinking and sober socials, such pub crawls, games nights, circling and more!

Be sure to follow our Instagram to keep up to date with our events!

Competitions

We attend comps such as LUBE, BUCS, and MUCC, and organise our own; Varsity, and NULSCC.

We also hold more relaxed internal comps, so there's something for everybody!

Trips

The great outdoors!

Recent destinations include the Peak District, Siurana in Spain, and Sicily in Italy.

Lessons

Fancy learning the ropes?

We offer top rope lessons in Term 1 and lead lessons in Term 2.

By joining, you will get:

Free access to:

  • The climbing wall during club hours
  • Gear hire (harnesses, shoes, rope, belay devices) during club hours
  • Top rope lessons (suitable for absolute beginners!) and lead climbing lessons

Cheaper Trips

Come along to bouldering daytrips, tours abroad, and sport climbing sessions at the crag!

Gear Discounts

Get a 10% discount at Lockwood's; a local gear shop and a great place to spend some money.

Weekly Emails

Get a summary of all club events, including socials, competitions and sign-ups for trips (socials and competitions are free to attend).
