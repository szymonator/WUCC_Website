Freshers Comp 2017

June 21, 2017

The Freshers Comp is an excellent opportunity for our climbers to put their bouldering skills to the test. There was a wide range of routes that were set for the competition to help improve different techniques. From technical balancing corners to powerful dyno moves. We encourage and generate a wide range of bouldering styles when it comes to solving problems.

Results:
Male 1st: Luke Collins
Male 2nd: Henry Smith
Male 3rd: Colin Tribe


Female 1st: Jenni French
Female 2nd: Monica Feito Kiczak
Female 3rd: Gabby Han
