Hello,

I'm Szymon Galutowski, the 2026/27 Media & Secretary for WUCC. This website was originally created by Vickie Snow in 2019/20, and then after a few years became unused and forgotten, and then went down after server hosting changes. When I came into the role, my goal was to once again make this a useful resource and hub of information for our current and prospective members. For instance, I removed proprietary software, incorporated our analytics, and made several design changes to align the website with Warwick Sport's brand vision.

Every single person listed below has made a massive contribution to the upkeep of the website, whether through helping us with hosting (a huge thanks to UWCS!), testing, or acting in an advisory role. For the future Media & Secretaries - good luck, and please don't break it!
