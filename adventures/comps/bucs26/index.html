BUCS 2026

February 14, 2026 | Gabriella Harrison

On a chilly Valentine's Day, the University of Warwick Climbing Team traveled to Climbing Works in Sheffield to compete in the annual BUCs Championships. While the members snuck in a gorgeous team breakfast in Sheffield, the captains sacrificed their morning to volunteer at the competition. In a massive gym with climbers from across the United Kingdom, the team warmed up and explored the wide variety of boulders set. Finally, after 3 hours of trying 30 climbs, the exhausted team made the long journey back home to Warwick.

We are extremely proud of Warwick’s performance at the 2026 BUCs championships, with three members (Josh Walmsley, Lena Jammes, Gabriella Harrison) placing in the top 100 of their category! The rest of the team (Zak Carter, Conor O’Brien, Madison Bracher) also performed spectacularly, placing within the top 120!

Warwick’s climbing team is looking forward to another successful BUCs Championship next year!
