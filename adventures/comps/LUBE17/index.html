LUBE 2016/17

June 21, 2017 | Sophie Davies

We participated in the London University Bouldering Event again this year which is a student run national bouldering competition, open to any universities.

LUBE has four rounds that take place at various venues across London. Each university can bring up to 8 climbers (2x teams of three members (mixed) and 2 individuals). 25 routes are set out and climbers are given three hours to complete as many routes as they can. Prizes are awarded to the top three men and women at each round and the overall top three men, women and university teams at the final round.

In the academic year of 2016/17 our Co-President Chloe Jones won overall 3rd for the female category, Dan Sayer came 9th out of the mens category and Warwick came 5th in the Competition.
