If you need emergency help please call campus security: 024 7652 2222 (on campus) or 999 (off campus).

You are also welcome to contact the current welfare officer (Ace) directly, either online or by having a chat at the wall. If you would prefer, you can also speak to our alternative welfare contact, Olivia. No pressure or minimum requirements, so please feel free to bring something up, whether it is big or small.

You can also anonymously contact our Welfare Officer (Ace) using the Welfare Form to raise any issues you'd like us to be aware of (with options to discuss, take action, or simply notify us).

Other Resources

Below is a list of links to resources you can access. Trying to find the right support can be overwhelming, especially in times of distress, so please remember the welfare officer can help guide you to support appropriate for your needs. Reaching out to someone you can trust is often a great first step.


General Mental Health Support

  • Warwick support access to therapy help
  • Warwick Support Homepage (To access the wellbeing portal)
  • Campaign Against Living Miserably (Late night helpline and webchat)
  • Mind A-Z of mental health (Mind also has lots of other helpful resources)
  • Papyrus (Suicide Prevention)
  • Student Minds (Student specific support)

Sexual Assault and Harrasment

  • Warwick report and support (submit a report of an incident if you wish, and also get direct one on one support)
  • What to do if you think your drink has been spiked
  • Coventry rape and sexual abuse centre (external to the university)
  • Rape Crisis England & Wales (helpline and live chat)

Support for marginalised groups

  • Stop Hate UK (for support around race or LGBTQ+ hate crimes)
  • Stand Against Racism & Inequality (more support for victims of hate crimes)
  • Scope (resources and helpline for disability)
