Introduction

Welcome to the Warwick University Climbing Club (WUCC) Privacy Policy. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This page outlines our data collection practices, how we utilize Google Analytics, and how you can exercise full control over your tracking preferences in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For the purposes of the GDPR, WUCC, operating under the Warwick Student Union (Warwick SU), acts as the legal Data Controller.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR, we must explicitly state our legal basis for holding and processing your personal data. Our legal bases are:

  • Consent: For operating Google Analytics and displaying photographs of members.
  • Performance of a Contract & Legitimate Interests: For collecting names, phone numbers, and emails during trip or tour registrations. We use this to fulfill our agreement to take you on the trip and to ensure member safety. We also rely on legitimate interests for collecting details during specific social events.

Third-Party Processors & International Transfers

To facilitate our club operations, we utilize third-party Data Processors such as Google (for Analytics and Forms). This means the personal data collected via our forms or tracking is transferred to and stored on servers located outside the UK, primarily in the United States. These international data transfers are protected under appropriate legal safeguards, specifically the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Google Analytics Tracking

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous statistics about how visitors interact with our website. This data helps us understand page popularity, device usage, and user journey patterns, which enables us to optimize our content and club information.

To protect your privacy, we have configured Google Analytics to:

  • Anonymize user IP addresses before logging or processing.
  • Only initiate tracking scripts after receiving your explicit opt-in consent.
  • Avoid collecting or correlating any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

Cookies & Storage

When you browse this site, the browser stores a small token in localStorage named wucc_cookie_consent to record whether you accepted or declined Google Analytics tracking. This prevents us from showing you the popup banner on every page load.

You can clear your browser storage or click the "Decline / Opt-Out" button in the consent settings above at any time to remove consent and stop tracking.

Photos & Event Personal Data

We display photos taken during club trips, tours, and social events on our website for historical and promotional purposes, and these are retained indefinitely or until consent is withdrawn. You have the right to request that any photo containing your likeness be taken down. Please contact us to submit a removal request.

When you register for trips or tours using our forms, we collect personal data including your name, phone number, and email address. To ensure safety and trip coordination, this data is retained temporarily and will be deleted within 30 days of the completion of the trip to allow for administrative safety reviews.

Your Data Subject Rights

You have the explicit right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Rectify/correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete the data.
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Data Portability (whether portable or not).

If you feel your data is being mishandled, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the UK, this authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, or would like to exercise any of your data rights, please contact our Media & Secretary or executive committee at mountaineering@warwicksu.com.
